# Commercial Policy, Reverse Trial & Licensing Architecture

## Overview
NativeLingo implements a client-side commercial model with zero token markups, based on the commercial strategy generated by `gpt-6-astra` in `astra_pricing_policy.md`.

## Open Source & Fair-Code Commercial Model
- **Open Codebase**: Code is public on GitHub for transparency, privacy verification, and local compiling.
- **Fair-Code Licensing**: Personal, hobbyist, and evaluation use is 100% free. Commercial and workplace use by businesses, agencies, and revenue-generating operations requires a commercial license under the NativeLingo EULA.
- **Official Binary Packaging**: Official signed Windows `.exe` installers with automatic updates and SLA support are monetized via Lemon Squeezy / Gumroad / Microsoft Store.

## Monetization Architecture
- **Model**: 14-Day Reverse Trial (No credit card required).
- **First-Run Behavior**: All users receive full Pro Edition access for 14 days immediately upon install.
- **Trial Expiration**: Automatically transitions to the permanently usable **NativeLingo Free Edition**. Never locks out or disables basic translation workflows.
- **Inference Philosophy**: 100% BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) and offline local AI via Ollama / LM Studio. NativeLingo charges $0 for AI tokens, and data is never sent through proxy relays.

## Pricing Tiers
- **Free Edition**: $0 forever. Includes unlimited translation hotkey, Slot 1 rewrite HUD preview + manual copy.
- **Pro Annual**: $69 / year ($5.75/month equivalent, 28% discount). Full in-place auto-paste (<250ms), 3 rewrite slots, 2 personal devices, all updates.
- **Pro Monthly**: $8 / month.
- **Pro Perpetual**: $149 one-time. Launch promotion of $119 for first 200 licenses. 12 months updates included, optional $49/year subsequent renewal.
- **Team Annual**: $99 / seat / year (min 3 seats). Centralized license management, corporate invoicing, priority support.

## Implementation Details
- **Engine**: `src/services/licenseService.js` manages persistent license state in `localStorage`:
  - `isPro`: Boolean flag indicating active trial or valid key.
  - `plan`: `'trial' | 'pro' | 'perpetual' | 'free'`.
  - `trialDaysRemaining`: Calculated dynamically from first-run timestamp.
  - Feature gates: `canUseAutoPaste()`, `canUseSlot(slotId)`, `canUseJargonExplainer()`.
- **UI Components**:
  - `src/components/SettingsModal.jsx`: Dedicated "Plan & License" sidebar tab with live status card, key activation input, tier comparison cards, and deactivation controls.
  - `src/components/Header.jsx`: Live status pill in header showing remaining trial days or Pro badge with one-click direct navigation to the Plan settings tab.
  - `PRICING.md`: Complete public pricing policy and terms of service.
