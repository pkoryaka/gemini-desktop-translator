## 1. Open Source Transparency & Commercial Model

NativeLingo follows a **Fair-Code / Source-Available Commercial Model** (similar to Obsidian, Aseprite, and Zed):

1. **Open Source for Trust & Security**: The entire codebase is public on GitHub. Anyone can inspect the implementation, audit how data is routed, or build it locally for personal and non-commercial evaluation.
2. **Commercial Licensing for Business**: In accordance with the [NativeLingo EULA](./LICENSE), using the software for business operations, commercial customer support, client communication, or corporate revenue generation requires an active **Pro** or **Team Commercial License**.
3. **Official Pre-Compiled Binaries**: Commercial licenses fund full-time development, code-signing certificates, automatic updates, and priority Windows desktop support.

## 4. The $0 Inference Markup Guarantee (BYOK)

Most modern AI apps charge $20 to $40 every month while secretly pocketing huge margins on API tokens or imposing artificial token ceilings.

**NativeLingo takes $0 on inference**:
- **Google Gemini Cloud**: Google provides an exceptionally generous free tier for Gemini API keys (up to 15 requests per minute, 1 million tokens/minute at zero cost). Even on paid pay-as-you-go tiers, typical daily translation costs less than $0.05 per month.
- **100% Offline Local AI**: Run open-source models via Ollama or LM Studio directly on your hardware (e.g., Llama 3, Qwen 2.5, Mistral, Gemma). Zero data leaves your machine, zero cost, 100% private.

## 5. Licensing & Device Policy

- **Personal Use**: Each Pro Annual and Perpetual license permits activation on up to **two (2) personal computers** owned or operated by the same licensee (e.g., a primary desktop and a laptop).
- **Seat Transfers**: You can deactivate a license on an old machine directly from `Settings > Plan & License` and activate it on a new computer instantly.
- **Offline License Verification**: Once activated, NativeLingo does not require constant internet connectivity to verify your license. Offline grace periods ensure your workflow is never interrupted on flights or remote locations.
